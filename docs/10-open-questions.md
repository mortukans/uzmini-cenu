# 10 Open questions

Decide before or during Phase 0.

1. **Name.** Working title "Uzmini Cenu". Alternatives: "Cik Maksā?",
   "Cenu Guru". Avoid anything with "SS" in it (trademark). Check `.lv`
   domain and App Store.
2. **Scoring feel.** Exponential 0-1000 vs Housle's "win within 5%".
   Playtest both in Phase 1 with 5 people.
3. **Show asking price or estimated sale price?** Asking price for v1
   (that is what the portal shows). Consider "sold for" mode later from
   land registry data.
4. **Anonymous play depth.** How many rounds before we ask for sign-in?
   Proposal: unlimited solo, sign-in for daily leaderboard and duels.
5. **Russian UI at launch?** Large audience in Riga. Cost is low with i18n
   from day one. Proposal: yes, machine-translate then proofread.
6. **Photo hosting.** Direct CDN links (legal-safe, fragile) vs cached
   copies (robust, riskier). Depends on Phase 0 probe and lawyer.
7. **Hint system in v1 or v1.1?** Proposal: v1.1 unless rewarded ads are
   wanted at launch.
8. **Region granularity.** Riga districts as their own filter? Proposal: yes
   for flats, since "Purvciems vs Centrs" is the whole game.
9. **Web landing page for daily share?** Proposal: static page showing
   today's blurred first photo and App Store link. Cheap, good for sharing.
10. **Partnership terms we would accept.** Logo placement, link-back, maybe
    revenue share on sponsored sets. What is the minimum we need in writing?
